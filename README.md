## Research standard

Every asset memorandum should:
- Lead with the investment thesis and explain the specific role the asset may play in financial or technological infrastructure.
- Separate independently verifiable facts, company statements, third-party reporting and Sub-Quadratic's interpretation.
- Prioritise primary sources: regulators, central banks, payment networks, statutory documents, company filings, technical documentation and on-chain data.
- Identify the team, governance, technology, customers, partnerships, acquisitions, integrations, token economics, competitive position and measurable adoption.
- Explain how value accrues to the token itself, not merely to the company, protocol or ecosystem.
- Track milestones and evidence that would strengthen or weaken the thesis.
- Date all research and metrics, preserve source links, and update material developments.

## Editorial position

Research is written from an investment-underwriting perspective. The objective is to build a clear, evidence-led case for why an asset merits capital allocation. Promotional claims are not treated as proof; token value capture must be demonstrated independently of company success.
